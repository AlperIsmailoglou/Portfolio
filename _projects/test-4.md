The outer body casing was sculptured using surface sweeps and smooth blend tools to achieve rounded aesthetic edges. Control elements were positioned across the front panel, including dual rotary dials, tactile buttons, and a central display screen. Functional elements such as the twin portafilters, cup warming rail, and lower drip tray grid were created as distinct solid features. Soft pink body coloring and metallic hardware accents were applied prior to final studio rendering.
