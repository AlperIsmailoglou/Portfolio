Modeling started by establishing a central symmetry axis and defining the main top and side boundary curves in Creo. Smooth surface lofting techniques formed fluid transitions along the knee recesses and front fork clearance area. The solid body was shelled to set internal volume and uniform wall thickness. Frame mounting brackets and a recessed filler neck with a twist-cap hardware piece were added to complete the build. A high-gloss metallic paint finish was applied for the final render to demonstrate surface continuity.
